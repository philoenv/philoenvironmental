Global Defense Innovation

Philo Environmental LLC was selected as a finalist in the NATO Innovation Challenge for engineering Project DADS (Distributed Airfield Denial System)—an edge-AI acoustic detection and tracking platform engineered for high-noise operational environments.

  • Real-time edge signal processing utilizing custom MFCC feature extraction and machine learning classifiers.
  • Designed for low-cost, scalable counter-unmanned observation and tactical field awareness.
  • Validated presentation and software architecture delivered to NATO HQ SACT evaluators.
