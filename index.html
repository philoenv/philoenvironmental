NATO Innovation Challenge Finalist
Global Defense Innovation

Philo Environmental LLC was selected as a finalist in the NATO Innovation Challenge 2026-2: Persistent Airfield Denial for engineering Project DADS (Distributed Airfield Denial System)—an autonomous acoustic detection and tracking platform engineered for high-noise operational environments.

  • Real-time, on-device signal processing utilizing custom MFCC feature extraction and machine learning classifiers.
  • Designed for low-cost, field-deployable counter-unmanned observation and tactical perimeter defense.
  • Validated presentation and software architecture delivered to NATO HQ SACT evaluators.
